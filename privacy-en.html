PRIVACY POLICY

Application: Scientific Calculator
Identifier: app.numerivelle.calculator
Version of this text: 27 September 2026

SUMMARY

This application collects no personal data, shows no advertising, contains no
analytics and offers no purchases. It does not hold the network permission: it
is technically unable to send anything off your device.

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

The publisher of the application is the data controller. Contact details are
shown on the application's Google Play listing and in section 9.

2. DATA COLLECTED

None. The application asks for no name, e-mail address, phone number or user
account. It reads no advertising identifier, no device identifier and no
location data.

3. WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Three things are stored locally, in the application's private storage:

  - your preferences (language, theme, detail level, angle unit);
  - the history of expressions you have entered;
  - your favourites.

None of it ever leaves the device. Android's automatic backup (a copy to your
Google account) and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this
application; the trade-off is that history and favourites are not carried
over when you change phones. You can erase all of it at any time from the
History screen, or by uninstalling the application, which removes its
storage entirely.

4. CAMERA AND IMAGES

The application reaches neither the camera nor your photos. Reading a
photographed question was withdrawn in version 1.5.0, and with it every line
of code that touched an image.

5. SHARING

The application transmits no data to third parties.

The Share and Copy buttons are the only ways anything leaves the app, and only
you can trigger them: Share hands the text you selected to the app you pick
(messaging, notes, mail); Copy places it on your device's clipboard. What that other app does with it is governed by its own privacy
policy.

6. THIRD-PARTY COMPONENTS

The application rests on Flutter and on the system's AndroidX libraries. It
contains no analytics, advertising or diagnostics library. In any case the
network permission is stripped from the installed package: without it Android
refuses the application any connection, whichever library might ask for one.
You can check this yourself in the Android settings, under the application's
permissions.

7. CHILDREN

The application is not directed at children under 13 and is not specifically
addressed to them. As no data is collected, no children's data is collected
either.

8. SECURITY AND RETENTION

Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server that could be breached.
Local data is protected by the Android application sandbox and, on recent
devices, by storage encryption. It is kept until you erase it.

9. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTACT

Since nothing is collected or held away from your device, there is nothing for
the publisher to disclose, correct, export or delete: the data is already in
your hands and you can destroy it at any moment. For any question about this
policy, write to taoufik.gassem@gmail.com (also published on the application's
Google Play listing).

If you live in the European Union or the European Economic Area, the General
Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you, among others, the rights of
access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. As
the publisher holds no data about you, these rights are exercised directly on
your device as described above. You may also lodge a complaint with the data
protection authority of your country (in France: the CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

10. CHANGES

Any change to this policy will be published together with a new version of the
application and a new date at the head of this text.
